# Banned by default

These are the marks of a video made by defaults. Each is banned unless `SPEC.md` names it as a deliberate choice
with a reason. When you see one on a sheet, it is a P1 (a P0 in a hero shot).

## Picture

- A dark void (near-black, featureless background) as the default ground.
- A glow, bloom or neon outline on type as decoration.
- Floating particles, bokeh dots or dust "for atmosphere".
- Lens flares; light leaks used as transitions everywhere.
- Spectrum bars, waveforms or a pulsing circle as the main picture.
- Gradients across the full spectrum; purple-to-blue "tech" gradients; mesh blobs drifting.
- Everything centred, every shot.
- A small object in the middle of an empty frame.
- Generic stock: hands on keyboards, city timelapses, drone shots of nowhere, people laughing at nothing.
- Generated images with the generator's own look (plastic skin, over-sharpened detail, orange-teal cast,
  symmetry, a "fantasy art" sheen), any text inside a generated image, hands in close-up.
- The same treatment for longer than about 6 s.

## Motion

- Everything fading in and out; opacity as the only animation.
- Every element on the same ease and the same duration.
- Ken Burns zoom on every still.
- A cut or a flash on every single beat.
- Shake on every hit; zoom-punch as the default emphasis.
- Bouncy springs on serious songs; springs on opacity or colour.
- Motion that starts and stops at the same time as the line (no anticipation, no follow-through).
- Wiggle/noise expressions on type for "energy".

## Type

- Default system fonts; Montserrat or Bebas Neue as the reflex choice; more than two families.
- Subtitle-style lyrics: white text in a bar at the bottom centre.
- Type under 2.6% of the short side; lines on screen shorter than they take to read.
- Typewriter reveal as the default.
- Words about the song instead of the song's own words ("feel the vibe").
- All caps everywhere with wide tracking.

## Edit and story

- A montage with no rule: unrelated pretty shots.
- A chorus that looks different every time without escalating.
- The ending that just fades to black.
- Starting on a black frame (the first frame is the thumbnail).
- Cutting in the middle of a sung word without a reason.

## Process

- Claiming "on beat", "in sync", "reviewed" or "licensed" without the file that proves it.
- Generating before the style frames are approved.
- Hand-typed lyric or beat times.
